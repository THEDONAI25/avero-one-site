Ask about Avero Digital Launch

Straight answers.

What happens after I sign up?

You get a free onboarding call to set your account up properly, plus two weeks of included support while you settle in.

Am I locked into a contract?

No. There are no lock-in contracts. Cancel anytime.

What happens after the first two weeks?

Support is included for your first two weeks. After that, ongoing support plans are available if you'd like a hand. Ask us and we'll tell you what fits.

Are there charges on top of my plan?

SMS and phone usage, WhatsApp and domain purchases are billed separately, by use. On Starter and Growth, AI tools are pay-per-use. Pro includes the AI Employee Growth plan with its monthly usage allowances; usage beyond that is billed per use. AI Unlimited upgrade available on every plan.

How are texts charged?

Texts are billed separately by use, so you only pay for what you send.

Can you move my existing contacts and data over?

Yes. Data migration is part of Avero Digital Launch, our done-for-you setup. Ask us for a quote.

How many people and contacts can I have?

Starter includes 2 users and 5,000 contacts, Growth 5 users and 25,000 contacts, and Pro 15 users and 100,000 contacts.

Do I need to be technical?

Most people don't need to be. We run your onboarding call, and Avero Digital Launch is there if you'd rather we configure everything for you.
